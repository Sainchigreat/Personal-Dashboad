## 6. Next Chapter — weekly production requirements

The live session remains Friday. The Next Chapter page should be the source of truth for session records and its checklist.

### Weekly creative assets (owned by the design team)
1. Main Next Chapter flyer.
2. Speaker flyer/bio design for the confirmed guest(s).
3. Separate advertising/promotional flyer explaining the session or series.

### Speaker register fields
For each confirmed or prospective speaker, record:
- Full name and preferred display name.
- Role/title and organisation.
- Short approved biography.
- Phone/WhatsApp and email/contact route.
- Status: prospective, invited, confirmed, date agreed, completed, or declined.

- Speaker-flyer status, approval and final asset link.
- Reminder dates and follow-up notes.

- Brief designer for main flyer, speaker flyer and separate ad flyer.

- Identify and contact three potential WhatsApp Status amplifiers daily during promotion.
- Prepare host script/run-of-show and audience questions.
- Test livestream/YouTube setup and confirm guest readiness.
- Host the Friday live session.
- Save recording, links and notes; publish/share replay and approved takeaways.

The existing Next Chapter session form already stores a guest field and session date/time, and session creation generates a checklist and linked tasks. A proper speaker register with contact details, multiple date options and asset approvals is a product enhancement; do not treat this planning document as if that database/UI has already been built.
